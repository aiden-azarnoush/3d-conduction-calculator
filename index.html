4. What happens on each face

Heat generated inside the block
Fixed temperature: the face is held at that value. Heat flux: heat pushed into the face (a heater, a chip). Insulated: nothing crosses. Convection: the face exchanges heat with a fluid at T∞ through the coefficient h; tick radiation to add ε σ (T⁴ − Tsur⁴), and sun to add absorbed sunlight α G (G ≈ 800 to 1000 W/m² in full sun).
